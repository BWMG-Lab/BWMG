What We Do

Research Areas

🦴

01 — Biomaterials

Biogenic Hydroxyapatite for Biomedical Applications

Engineering hydroxyapatite from Nile tilapia bones, fish scales, and other bio-waste for bone scaffolds, implant coatings, and tissue engineering. Studying sintering behavior, phase transitions, and vacancy formation.

Nile Tilapia Bone HAp / TCP Bioactivity Scaffold
⚡

02 — Energy Storage

Bio-derived Materials for Batteries & Supercapacitors

Porous electrospun carbon nanofibers bearing TiO₂ hollow nanospheres derived from biogenic precursors — engineered for high-performance supercapacitor electrodes and next-generation energy storage.

Carbon Nanofibers TiO₂ Electrospinning Supercapacitor
📡

03 — Sensors

Functional Materials for Environmental & Biosensing

Developing bio-waste-derived functional oxides with tailored electrical and optical properties for detecting environmental pollutants and biosignals in real-world applications.

Metal Oxides Electrical Props. Environmental Biosensing
🔬

04 — Characterization

Synchrotron-Based Advanced Structure Analysis

Local atomic structure analysis via synchrotron X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XANES, EXAFS) and diffraction at SLRI Thailand — revealing atomic-scale insights in bio-derived materials.

SLRI Thailand XANES / EXAFS XRD Local Structure
🖨️

05 — Additive Manufacturing

3D-Printed Bioceramic Scaffolds

Fabricating patient-specific bone scaffolds via 3D printing using biogenic calcium phosphate powders — investigating HAp/TCP ratios, porosity, and mechanical performance for clinical translation.

3D Printing HAp/TCP Ratio Porosity Tissue Engineering
💻

06 — Computational

First-Principles DFT Calculations

Density functional theory studies on elastic, mechanical, optical, and magnetic properties of bio-derived and functional materials — bridging atomistic simulation with experimental characterization.

DFT Sr / Mg Doped HAp Elastic Properties Optical Props.
